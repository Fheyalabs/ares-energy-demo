What is real and what is not. Real: threshold CKKS keygen, homomorphic aggregation, the comparator, threshold decryption, and every participant's allocation derived locally from the public clearing price. Simulated: encryption happens server-side — a browser cannot yet do it because OpenFHE is not compiled to WebAssembly. Also, both decryption shares live in one process here; in deployment they sit with separate institutions.
